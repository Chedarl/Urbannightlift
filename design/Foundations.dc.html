Urban Night Lift

Foundations, corrected

Three defects found in the live token system. None of them is a matter of taste — each one is checkable, and two of them cost legibility or meaning on the money screens.

01

A price and a warning are the same colour

--color-caution and --color-gold-400 are both #d4af37 — byte-identical. On the review screen the amount owed and the warning that a shop charged over the cap render in exactly the same gold.

Now — indistinguishable

Proposed — gold is money, amber is caution
Delivery fee 1 950 XAF
The shop charged 2 400 over your cap.
gold-400 #d4af37 · money & brand only
caution #f2a03d · warmer, always with an icon
restricted #e0522f · unchanged

The rule that makes it hold under colour-blindness and a dim screen: a caution always carries an icon and a left rule; money never does. Colour alone was never going to be enough.

02

Eight card surfaces, no ladder

Cards are drawn with eight different treatments across the app — bg-ink-800, bg-ink-900, bg-ink-900/50, /60, /40 — so nothing reliably reads as raised. Four named steps, and the inset top highlight that makes depth legible on near-black.

surface-1 #110b1c resting card
surface-2 #1a1130 sheet, active
surface-3 #241845 popover
03

365 hand-typed font sizes, and a 10px floor

text-[11px] appears 282 times and text-[10px] 72 more. That is not a scale, and 10px is below comfortable reading on a phone at 1 AM — which is this product's entire context. Seven steps, and nothing below 13px.
