# Homepage character art — how to add African-anime characters

The homepage hero is a code-built animated Yaoundé-night scene. It also has a
**drop-in slot** for character art: if a file exists at **`public/hero-character.png`**,
it renders (gently floating) on the right side of the hero. No code change needed —
just add the file and redeploy.

## Requirements
- **File name:** `hero-character.png` (lowercase), placed in `public/`.
- **Format:** PNG with a **transparent background** (so it blends into the night scene).
- **Orientation:** portrait, character roughly full-height. Tall/vertical works best.
- **Size:** ~1000×1400 px is plenty. Keep under ~1.5 MB.

## AI prompts you can use
Generate with any image tool (Midjourney, Leonardo, DALL·E, Firefly, Stable Diffusion).
Ask for a **transparent background** (or remove it afterwards with remove.bg).

**Rider character**
> Full-body anime illustration of a confident young Cameroonian delivery rider at night,
> wearing a purple and gold jacket and a helmet under their arm, standing beside a
> motorbike, neon purple and gold rim lighting, deep black background, modern premium
> anime style, clean line art, transparent background, portrait orientation.

**Customer character**
> Anime illustration of a stylish young African woman at night receiving a delivery,
> warm smile, purple and gold accent lighting, deep black background, sleek modern anime
> style, transparent background, portrait orientation.

**Duo / brand scene (optional wider art)**
> Two young Cameroonian characters — a delivery rider and a happy customer — anime style,
> night city of Yaoundé with neon purple and gold glow, crescent moon, cinematic and
> premium, transparent background.

## Tips
- Keep the palette **purple + gold on black** to match the brand.
- 1–2 characters read best; avoid busy backgrounds (the scene is already animated).
- Want different characters for different pages later? Tell me and I'll add more slots
  (e.g. per-service order pages).
